    Expected vs. actual units

    Expected = what the closing line says these bets were worth (stake × CLV). Actual = what they won or lost. The two converge over a long run; a gap over a short one is variance.

    Every pick

    CLV EV is the expected profit per unit staked if the no-vig closing line is the true price; betting the close itself scores about −2% to −4.5%. Spread CLV accounts for key numbers (a point through 3 is worth about four times a point through 5). Formulas: clv/odds_math.py.
